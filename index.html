Reportability Triage

Assess seriousness event by event against the six ICH E2A criteria, derive case-level seriousness, check expectedness against the correct reference safety information and causality, then see whether the case is a SUSAR (trials) or which postmarket clock applies.

Setting

Reference safety information: the Reference Safety Information section of the Investigator's Brochure — not the whole IB (§10.2).

Events

Seriousness is assigned per event; the case is serious if any event is serious (§6.2). Up to three events.

Book Ch. 7 · §7.2–7.4

Severity ≠ Seriousness

Severity is the intensity of the event, assigned by the reporter (or per the protocol's grading scale). Seriousness is the sponsor's regulatory classification against ICH E2A. The two are independent.
